07 · Diligence

Get margin and a holdout before you scale.

08 / 08

The panel can price a household. It cannot tell you what that household is worth.

01 / MARGIN

Ask for contribution margin per household.

Without it, $2.06 stays a cost. A 20%-margin item and a 60%-margin item look the same here.

02 / HOLDOUT

Test the reach lift against a holdout.

Promotions are scheduled, not randomized. A holdout or geo test would turn +76.5% reach into a causal number.

03 / CUSTOMER KEY

Link households across weeks.

No customer ID means reach cannot be split into new buyers and switchers. Retention and LTV need a key this panel does not have.

What the panel is

524,950 store–product–week records · 156 weeks · 77 stores × 55 products · 4 categories · 9 metro areas.

What it cannot say

No customer key. No cost or margin field. No randomized promotion assignment.

Start with the $2.06. Then test whether the margin clears it.
Methods: HAC(4), season-adjusted OLS, Mann–Kendall; 2,000-shuffle permutation; Fisher-z with Benjamini–Hochberg; cell + week fixed effects with cell-clustered SEs; cluster bootstrap over product–store pairs. Full audit in notebooks/EDA.ipynb. Prepared October 2026
