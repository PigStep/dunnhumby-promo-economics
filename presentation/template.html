07 · Diligence

Get margin and a holdout before you scale.

08 / 08

The panel can price a household. It cannot tell you what that household is worth.

01 / MARGIN

Ask for contribution margin per household.

Without it, {{COST}} stays a cost. A 20%-margin item and a 60%-margin item look the same here.

02 / HOLDOUT

Test the reach lift against a holdout.

Promotions are scheduled, not randomized. A holdout or geo test would turn {{REACH}} reach into a causal number.

03 / CUSTOMER KEY

Link households across weeks.

No customer ID means reach cannot be split into new buyers and switchers. Retention and LTV need a key this panel does not have.

What the panel is

No customer key. No cost or margin field. No randomized promotion assignment.
