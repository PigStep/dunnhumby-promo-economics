07 · Diligence

De-risk before you scale.

08 / 08

The panel can measure reach. It cannot prove new customers, profit, or clearance.

01 / HOLDOUT

Test feature + display against a silent price cut.

Promotions are scheduled, not randomized. A holdout or geo test would turn {{REACH}} reach into a causal number.

02 / MARGIN

Get contribution margin and COGS.

No margin field here: a 20%-margin item and a 60%-margin item score the same. Price cuts for clearance need inventory data too.

03 / CUSTOMER KEY

Link households across weeks.

Without a key, reach cannot be split into new buyers, repeat buyers, and switchers. Reach is not CAC, and there is no LTV.

What the panel is

{{N_ROWS}} store–product–week records · {{N_WEEKS}} weeks · {{N_STORES}} stores × {{N_UPCS}} products · {{N_CATEGORIES}} categories · {{N_MSAS}} metro areas.

What it cannot say

No customer key. No cost, margin, or inventory field. No randomized promotion assignment.

For reach, visibility. For clearance, price. This panel can prove the first — test the second.
Methods: HAC(4), season-adjusted OLS, Mann–Kendall; 2,000-shuffle permutation; Fisher-z with Benjamini–Hochberg; cell + week fixed effects with cell-clustered SEs; cluster bootstrap over product–store pairs. Full audit in notebooks/EDA.ipynb. Prepared October 2026
