07 · What to underwrite

Underwrite reach — and get the margin data before underwriting profit.

08 / 08

The same discount returns {{PB_MEDIAN}}× to {{PB_OBS}}× of incremental revenue per $1 depending on how promo weeks are credited. None of these is a profit number.

01 / LEVER

Move spend from silent price cuts to communicated offers.

Feature + display: {{MECH_FD}} reach. A price cut alone: {{MECH_TPR}}. The mechanism, not the markdown, is where the variance is.

02 / PRICE

Use category economics, not the blended average.

One household costs {{COST_BAG}} in bag snacks and {{COST_PIZZA}} in frozen pizza. The four category intervals do not overlap.

03 / GUARDRAIL

Do not read payback as profit.

The ratio ranges from {{PB_MEDIAN}}× to {{PB_OBS}}×; {{PB_CLEAR}} of pairs clear break-even on their own observed ratio. No cost field means no margin.
